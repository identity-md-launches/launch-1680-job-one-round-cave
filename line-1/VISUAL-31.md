# Visual review — round 31

The round-30 wall was recovered from its content-addressed image and
SHA-256-verified before any mark was added. I used the installed image editor
to study a compact charcoal-and-ochre carried-bundle and stone-seal motif, then
assembled a deliberately bounded dry-brush version in unused upper-center
rock. The final assembly changes no ancestor pixels outside its 145 × 90 pixel
addition box.

The finished wall is a 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG; its companion is a 512 × 512
baseline JPEG. I visually reviewed the PNG at full size. It retains the warm,
rough torch-lit cave framing, the large and small inherited wide-mouthed,
heavy-lidded Pepe figures, and their visible five-digit hands. The new mark is
an unlettered bundle, seal, and small stones only: it adds no hands, handprints,
text, numbers, caption, logo, signature, watermark, border, or frame.

`visual-checks-31.json` records dimensions, formats, hashes, changed-pixel
counts, and the visual requirement result. Unmet visual requirements: none
observed. The identity and digit count of inherited hand marks remain a visual
judgment; no new hand was introduced.
